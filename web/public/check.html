Levels of attention

Results are grouped by the attention they deserve from you. Fix this: a likely error in the reference, such as fields from two different papers or a retracted work. Worth a look: a substantive difference you may want to correct, such as the online-first year of a paper that now has a volume and issue. Probably fine: a difference among the published versions of the work, such as a reprint, a later edition, or a handbook chapter with separate print and online years. Can't check here: a kind of source beyond the registries' coverage, or a source that the registries lack; treat the missing match as neutral.

The verdicts and their tiers

Fail-closed means that RefGuard returns uncertain references to you for review and withholds a pass. A reference passes only when the registries recognize it.

Fabricated candidate
Blocking. Both Crossref and doi.org return 404 for the DOI.
Inconsistent
Blocking. The paper exists, but the cited title, authors, year, venue or DOI conflict with the paper's record. RefGuard gives this verdict to references that mix fields from two papers.
Retracted
Blocking. The registry marks the work as retracted.
Benign drift
Review. RefGuard found the right record with a small difference: a citation of the online-first year of a paper that now has a print year, a year off by one for a reason other than the gap between online and print dates, a changed subtitle, or an incomplete author list.
Ambiguous match
Review. RefGuard found a record, but without a DOI to anchor the match, the record may be the wrong one: the record's authors all differ from yours, or the record's year differs from yours by two or more years. Check which record or edition you cited.
Unverified
Review. The registries left the reference unconfirmed, which is a weaker finding than fabrication. If the search found only a different work, the note names it. Theses, reports and trade press are unindexed, and the note says so.
Registry unreachable
Review. RefGuard lost its connection to the registry and so leaves the reference uncertified.
Consistent
Pass. The cited fields all match the registry record.

RefGuard checks whether your references accurately describe real papers; the choice of the right paper to cite remains your judgment. RefGuard takes its registry data from Crossref, Open Library and doi.org.
