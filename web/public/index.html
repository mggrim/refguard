RefGuard

Paste a bibliography (BibTeX or a plain reference list). Every entry is checked against the OpenAlex registry: does the paper exist, do the title, authors, year, venue, and DOI belong to it, and has it been retracted? No AI involved — pure registry lookup and comparison.

Verdicts: FABRICATED_CANDIDATE = the DOI is registered nowhere (checked at OpenAlex and doi.org). INCONSISTENT = the entry's fields don't match the real paper (wrong authors, year, venue, or mixed-up fields). RETRACTED = the registry marks the paper retracted. BENIGN_DRIFT = formatting or online-first/print differences. UNVERIFIED = not found anywhere; check by hand. REGISTRY_UNREACHABLE = network problem, not evidence of fabrication — the check fails closed. RefGuard verifies that entries describe real papers accurately; it cannot judge whether they are the right papers to cite. Adapted from verification work by Katie Apker, Pedro Sant'Anna, Scott Cunningham, and Dhuliawala et al. (2023, Chain-of-Verification).
